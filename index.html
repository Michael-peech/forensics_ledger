System Protocol Guide

Standard Operating Procedures for the CYB-305 Immutable Ledger.

1. Identity & Access Control (RBAC)

Access to evidence is strictly governed by the smart contract based on your assigned clearance:

  • Role 1 (Investigator): Authorized to seize and log initial evidence.
  • Role 2 (Clerk): Authorized to receive and vault physical assets.
  • Role 3 (Lab Tech): Authorized to check out assets and encode forensic matrices.
  • Role 4 (Liaison): Authorized to extract assets for legal/court review.

2. Module A: Initial Intake

Used strictly when discovering new evidence in the field. Uploading a file generates a decentralized Walrus Blob ID. The blockchain only stores this hash, ensuring zero-knowledge privacy while guaranteeing the file is never tampered with.

3. Module B: Custody Transfer

The smart contract mathematically rejects any transfer to an operative who lacks the required clearance for the selected action. Utilize the dynamic terminal autocomplete to locate registered operatives.
